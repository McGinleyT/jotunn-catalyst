Head Empty, Only Jötunn! [JÖTN]

Who we are

We are a clan of Destiny 2 veterans from all across the US. Our roots go back to LFG raiding back in Destiny 1. Major accomplishments include contest mode Root of Nightmares, Petra's Run completion, many solo flawless dungeon completions (despite having no merit), and Nezarec Sublime Pantheon completion. We have a combined total of over 1,200 raid completions and over 1,500 dungeon completions.
