Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Jötunn Catalyst (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates https://jotunncatalyst.com. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection of personal information.

 

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website, how we use website analytics, and how third-party infrastructure providers support our website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information through user tracking, advertising profiles, or cross-site behavioral advertising.

 

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be processed by our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, including information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, request metadata, and information about the country of origin of traffic.

 

If you contact us or voluntarily provide information through a form or email, we may use that information to respond to your inquiry.

3. Website Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Real User Monitoring, or RUM) and Cloudflare Zone Analytics to understand website performance and traffic. These services provide different types of information.

 

Cloudflare Web Analytics / RUM

This service measures website performance as experienced by visitors' browsers. It may collect technical performance measurements, page-loading information, and related metadata, such as the referring page and browser characteristics.

 

According to Cloudflare's documentation, its Web Analytics RUM beacon does not use cookies or browser storage for analytics and discards the source IP address at the nearest Cloudflare data center rather than storing it in its core analytics databases or logs.

 

Cloudflare Zone Analytics

This service analyzes HTTP traffic processed through Cloudflare's network. We use it to view statistics such as request volumes, estimated unique visitors, and the countries from which traffic originates.

 

Cloudflare uses the IP address associated with a request to determine its originating country. Its unique-visitor metric can count unique IP addresses during the relevant reporting period. Consequently, these statistics may involve the processing of IP addresses and associated technical request information by Cloudflare.

 

We use these analytics to understand general website usage, assess traffic patterns, and maintain and improve our website. We do not use them to create advertising profiles or track visitors across unrelated websites.

 

Analytics figures are estimates and may include automated traffic, depending on the metric and configuration.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We do not intentionally use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or browser fingerprinting to build visitor profiles.

 

Cloudflare Web Analytics RUM does not use cookies or browser storage for its analytics collection. However, Cloudflare's other infrastructure and security features may use cookies or similar technologies when required for functions such as security, traffic management, or abuse prevention.

 

The use of such technologies depends on the Cloudflare services enabled and our configuration.

5. How We Use Information

Technical information and analytics may be used to:

 

  • Operate, maintain, and secure our website.
  • Monitor website performance and reliability.
  • Understand aggregate traffic volumes and country-level traffic distribution.
  • Estimate unique visitors and identify general usage patterns.
  • Detect, investigate, and mitigate malicious or abusive traffic.
  • Respond to inquiries submitted directly to us.

 

We do not sell personal information or use our website analytics for targeted advertising.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Cloudflare, Inc. for DNS and website infrastructure services, including analytics and, where applicable, security and performance functions.

 

Cloudflare may process technical information and website request data to provide these services. The information Cloudflare processes, its retention, and its handling of that information depend on the services used and applicable configurations and terms.

 

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics Documentation
  • Cloudflare Zone Analytics Documentation

7. Data Retention and Security

We do not intentionally maintain individual visitor profiles for advertising or behavioral tracking.

 

Cloudflare may retain technical request data, analytics records, and aggregated statistics according to its applicable service-specific retention practices. We do not independently control all retention periods for data processed by Cloudflare.

 

We take reasonable steps appropriate to our website to protect information under our control. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. U.S. Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence and the applicability of relevant privacy laws, you may have rights regarding personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information, or to opt out of certain types of processing.

 

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising as those terms are generally used in applicable U.S. state privacy laws. Whether other obligations or rights apply depends on the relevant law and our activities.

 

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the details in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, analytics configuration, service providers, or legal obligations change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
